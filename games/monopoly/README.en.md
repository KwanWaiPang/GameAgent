# World Tour

- A physical-board style loop: buy world cities, collect rent, upgrade, and play against a local computer.
- The board uses color groups, flags, four corner squares, and a title in the center. The city list is original to the hub.

## Play

- Roll and move clockwise. Passing Go pays $2000.
- Buy empty lots. Upgrade your own lots (house → villa → hotel).
- Chance / fate, tax, station flights, jail, and an Alpine holiday
- The sidebar sets starting cash and the number of humans and computers (2–4 seats in total)
