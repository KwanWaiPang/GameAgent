How the Highland Cup works

Chasers (3) pass and throw the red Quaffle through a golden hoop for 10 points. Keepers guard the hoops. Beaters (2) swing at the two Bludgers to knock rival riders off line. The Seeker hunts the Golden Snitch; catching it scores 150 and ends the match.

The Snitch is released after 45 seconds, or press “Release the snitch”. Drag to orbit, wheel or pinch to zoom, click a rider to follow them. Space boosts the Emberwing Seeker (8 s cooldown). Wind drifts riders, balls and pennants.
